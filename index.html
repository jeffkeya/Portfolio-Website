Where the two meet

Community protection work and software both come down to the same thing: building something reliable enough that people can trust it. A referral pathway and a database schema are both, at heart, systems for making sure nothing important falls through the cracks.

My field attachments taught me to work carefully with confidential information, coordinate across stakeholders, and follow through on every case. My technical training taught me the same discipline — attention to detail, accuracy, and accountability — applied to code and data instead of case files.

That's the strength I bring to any fast-paced, multidisciplinary environment: analytical thinking paired with genuine care for the person the work is meant to serve.
